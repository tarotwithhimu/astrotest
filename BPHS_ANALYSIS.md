# Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra guide and chart tools

This note describes the BPHS-derived features in Jyotish Kundli, using the English PDF supplied with the project. The offline guide summarizes all 97 chapters and indexes selected source rules, tables and tool ideas. It is a searchable study aid—not a claim that every instruction, yoga or prediction in the book has been implemented as software. Chapter descriptions are paraphrased rather than reproduced verbatim.

## Implemented features

The **BPHS Guide** contains 97 concise chapter summaries and a searchable index of rule families, tables, result-bearing yoga records and implementation cautions. The chart **Analysis** section calculates selected Moon/Sun combinations, Nabhash patterns and Panchamahapurusha formations; when a supported yoga is detected, its historical result and stated qualifications are shown with source chapter/verse attribution; non-sensitive wording is retained, while explicit health, longevity and mortality claims are omitted and identified as redactions. It also includes a sixteen-varga subject/dignity matrix. **More** includes the seven-graha Ashtakavarga and separate Lagna BAV, plus Trikona and Ekadhipatya Shodhana worksheets. The bhava-lord, graha-aspect, rāśi-aspect, lordship and sign-dignity tables remain available.

The **Jaimini** tab now includes the app's eight Chara Karakas (including Rahu with reversed degree) and the Atmakaraka's Karakamsa sign, twelve Arudha/Bhava Padas, a sign-based Argala/Virodhargala occupancy table, and a BPHS/Neelakantha-style Chara Dasha timeline. The **Chakras** tab includes a three-ring Sudarshana Chakra and a diagrammatic Pataki Rishta vedha map. Pataki displays natal whole-sign houses, source-rule links to Lagna/Moon, the Dhoom-derived Upagraha chain and Gulika (Mandi); it does not generate adverse-event predictions.

These additions calculate placements, counts and timing. Varga exalted/own/debilitated labels are only sign-state markers, not Vimshopaka Bala or Shadbala. Ashtakavarga reductions stop before Pinda calculations. The Pataki chart is not a complete PRC timing calculation. The tools do not generate a complete Jaimini or Sudarshana prediction for every period, house and graha. Yoga results are explicitly labeled as historical text, not predictions. Clause-level display filtering omits explicit health, longevity and mortality claims while retaining non-sensitive clauses, source qualifications and citations; the underlying indexed source data remains bundled for study.

## Conventions and limits

**Bhava Padas (BPHS Ch. 29, vv. 1–5).** Each Pada uses the double-count from the parent sign to its classical seven-graha sign lord. The app follows the supplied BPHS edition's special cases: a preliminary Pada on the parent sign is moved to the tenth from that house; one on the seventh is moved to the fourth. If the lord is in the fourth from the house, the resulting correction is its occupied sign. Some modern commentaries apply a different correction, including the tenth for both cases; the app names its selected edition-specific rule in every affected row. Scorpio and Aquarius co-lord strength is not independently ranked in this Pada table; it uses the app's standard classical sign-lord map.

**Argala (BPHS Ch. 31, vv. 2–9).** The table shows classical-graha placements for the primary 2nd/12th, 4th/10th and 11th/3rd pairs and the secondary 5th/9th pair, for each target sign. A count comparison is only a quick reference: the source also requires strength assessment and discusses special cases. Rahu and Ketu are omitted because the text's reverse-count handling is not implemented. The table does not infer a life event or final Argala judgment.

**Chara Dasha.** Lineages vary, so the app labels one BPHS/Neelakantha-style convention rather than presenting it as universal. It starts from natal Lagna; the odd/even nature of the ninth sign selects the forward/reverse twelve-sign major-period order. For each sign, duration counts inclusively to its lord in the Savya/Apasavya direction and subtracts one; own-sign periods are assigned twelve years. Scorpio/Aquarius use the dual-lord choice and sign-strength factors described in the supplied BPHS Ch. 46, vv. 158–167. Dignity adjustments and the one-to-twelve-year duration bound are explicit software conventions. Calendar labels use 365.25 days per year, the twelve-sign sequence is repeated for successive cycles, and antardashas are omitted because the consulted procedures disagree on their sequence or do not define it consistently. The timeline lists placements, associated padas/karakas and dates; period activation is not a guaranteed event.

**Sudarshana Chakra (BPHS Ch. 74, vv. 4–28).** All three rings share the same house numbering while their starting signs differ: natal Lagna, Moon sign and Sun sign. The table and circle show signs and graha placements; they do not automatically deliver the chapter's full outcome statements or assess every aspect/strength. The current bhava periods use a 360-day year divided into twelve 30-day months, with 2.5-day pratyantar steps to match the durations stated in the supplied edition. This is a stated timing convention, not a claim that every modern calendar implementation uses the same conversion. The chapter's caution for repeated anchor signs is surfaced in the UI.

**Pataki Rishta Chakra.** The twelve-house line diagram uses the K. N. Rao memory-table rule: movable signs give vedha to the 1st/6th/9th/12th, fixed to the 1st/4th/7th/10th and dual to the 1st/2nd/5th/8th from themselves. It reports which bodies match those patterns to natal Lagna and Moon, without grading harm/protection or predicting illness, disability, longevity or death. Dhoom and the derived Vyatipat, Parivesh, Chap (Indradhanus) and Upaketu follow BPHS Ch. 3, vv. 61–64. The text characterizes Upagrahas as malefic; this tool intentionally shows computed positions and source provenance only, not individual harm predictions. Gulika's longitude is the ascendant at the start of Saturn's one-eighth day/night segment per BPHS Ch. 3, vv. 66–70, using the app's approximate sunrise/sunset model and a fixed UTC offset. Dandesh/Ardhayamesh, the book's age-specific schedule and complete PRC judgment are not implemented; PRC, standard annual Tripataki progression and the current natal diagram should not be treated as interchangeable.

**Other limits.** The calculations use the app's existing D1 sign placements and Lahiri sidereal chart. They do not include every BPHS yoga, every cancellation or strength rule, full Shadbala/Vimshopaka, all divisional-chart confirmations, complete Argala strength resolution, or a source-complete event prediction for every Dasha. The chapter guide is an index and concise summary, not a substitute for the source text or a full translation. Sensitive historical health and mortality claims are not displayed as personal predictions; other traditional statements are likewise labeled as historical claims, not actionable advice.

## Sources

The primary source is the user-supplied *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* English PDF, especially Ch. 3 (Upagrahas), Ch. 7 (Vargas), Ch. 29 (Bhava Padas), Ch. 31 (Argala), Ch. 46 (sign-Dasha rules), Ch. 66–68 (Ashtakavarga and Shodhana), Ch. 74 (Sudarshana Chakra) and Ch. 75 (Panchamahapurusha). Verse numbering and terminology can vary between editions.

The Pataki reference is K. N. Rao, *Pataki Rishta Chakra*, excerpts at [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/551522041/Jyotish-2004-Pataki-Rishta-Chakra-K-N-Rao). The square-grid construction and ordinary Tripataki annual progression are described separately by [Dr. Shanker Adawal](https://tagtoadawal.blogspot.com/2013/01/encyclopedia-of-vedic-astrology-tajik_29.html). They are related methods with differing procedures, not a single universal rule set.

For the named Chara Dasha convention and its known variants, the app cross-checked U.K. Jha, “What Is The Correct Procedure for Computing Jaimini’s Chara Dasa?” [Saptarishis Astrology](https://saptarishisshop.com/what-is-the-correct-procedure-for-computing-jaiminis-chara-dasa-by-uk-jha/), and “Jaimini Astrology: Chara Dashas” [Jothishi](https://jothishi.com/jaimini-astrology-chara-dashas/). The latter is used for the stated Savya/Apasavya counting groups, not to infer disputed subperiods.

The differing Arudha exception is discussed in [Paramarsh's Jaimini Pada Lagnas guide](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/jaimini-astrology/jaimini-pada-lagnas); its convention is not substituted for the supplied BPHS edition. Argala pairs were also cross-checked against Sanjay Rath, [“Argala: Planetary Intervention”](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/amateur/argala-planetary-intervention/).
